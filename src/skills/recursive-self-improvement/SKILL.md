# Recursive Self-Improvement

Improve how future research is done, and how future improvements are selected.
The persistent successor is semantic `research-memory`, not edited application
source, installed Agents/Skills, or model weights. This Skill is orchestration
only: specialists own research artifacts and execution.

## Contract Before Extra Work

Use `research-experience` for selective recall, exact active reads, applicability,
proposals and independent verification. Ordinary tasks may yield learning from
existing evidence; they do not authorize extra practice. For an authorized RSI/
auto/overnight campaign, record in the session:

- target outcome, failure to diagnose, and method or strategy being changed;
- frozen active `memory_refs` (or `[]`), baseline and isolated candidate;
- fixed model, tools, environment, evaluator, source/data boundary and holdout;
- allowed specialist outputs, mutable scope, total budget and verification reserve;
- acceptance/regression criteria and stop conditions.

Budget includes all attempts, failures, diagnosis and verification. Stop on the
agreed budget, target, user Stop, repeated infrastructure failure, or no valid
next task. Missing authority is a normal workflow block; learning is not a reason
to extend a completed task.

## Choose The Evidence-Producing Loop

| Need | Dispatch and consolidation |
|---|---|
| Several independent gaps | **Broad wave:** designate members and non-overlapping outputs before launch; every member receives the same frozen starting memory. Inspect arriving evidence, but wait for **all designated terminal outcomes** before any learning consolidation, candidate ranking, verification selection or publication. Consolidate sequentially in a recorded order. |
| Attributed failure or fragile success | **Deep practice:** choose one task that distinguishes rival explanations, check a local diagnostic first, then replay/regression and end-to-end performance. After independently verified activation, retry the unchanged target in a fresh context with the successor pin. |
| Suspected improvement-policy gain | **Strategy comparison:** freeze inherited and candidate strategies; measure generation/diagnosis/selection quality at matched total budget, not merely a better paper score. |

A runtime Error closes a wave member as **unverified infrastructure output**, not
a scientific negative or convergence. No handoff is fabricated. A still-Working
member leaves the barrier open. At the deadline, stop further work through existing
ownership/cancellation paths and record actual terminal or unresolved states;
never pretend cancellation has settled. A partial wave can close without a
learning claim. Preserve the same frozen set despite newer live memory.

A deep retry is fresh (`agent:"search"`, for example), not the author's continued
session. Productive practice needs a new discriminating question and budget; it
cannot disguise another retry of the same infrastructure failure. The existing
one-targeted-correction limit still applies.

## Verify, Freeze And Inherit

Before any publication or strategy comparison, read
[verification.md](references/verification.md). Require independent-session evidence
in the pending author's exact cwd. Inspect actual reports before activation;
fail/inconclusive leaves the incumbent. Resolve or explicitly park pending work
as inert at closure; never pass it to the next ordinary task.

After activation, send the exact returned active snapshot to a fresh task. Its
handoff records **which strategy decision was actually used**, resulting candidate
lineage, costs and regressions. Further proposals use that active pin in `basedOn`.
Held-out final evaluation feedback does not feed this campaign's learning.

## Preserve Owners And Completion

- `autoresearch` is the inner loop: **one Experiment owner per campaign**, not a
  new child per trial. A separate fresh verifier is a bounded subsequent task.
- Search verifies retrieval methods; Experiment verifies empirical methods;
  Writing/Figures verify their own craft. Review remains manuscript/source
  critique with immutable reports, no experiments/source edits, and no automatic
  second review. An RSI label does not bypass that limit.
- Research Assistant inspects evidence and manages semantic versions. It never
  produces specialist source/review/experiment artifacts. Its own strategy needs
  another session's verification; a responsible specialist can test that strategy
  within its allowed work.
- Return normal task status plus memory provenance, observed failures, budget,
  and pending disposition. **No useful learning can still be complete.** A stored
  successor or a small favorable comparison proves neither statistical efficacy
  nor accelerating recursive improvement.
